Operation Vineyard · Tuscany, 1944

Oberst Krause runs the German post at the vineyard farm and keeps his routine: the villa terrace, his field radio at the truck, the yard. Kill him, then get back to the hill you started from. Wreck his radio first and no help can be called; his papers are worth taking. Use the planes, the artillery and the generator to cover your shots.
