Operation Vineyard · Tuscany, 1944

Oberst Krause runs the German post at the vineyard farm and keeps his routine: the villa terrace, his field radio at the truck, the yard. Kill him, then get back to the hill you started from.

  • Eliminate Oberst Krause. Tag him with the binoculars to follow him.
  • Optional: wreck his field radio first and no reinforcements can be called.
  • Optional: take his papers from his body.
  • Optional: destroy the Panzer in the yard (AP rounds or a satchel).
  • Optional: open the safe in the Oberst's office upstairs in the villa and take his orders. Find the combination (it's written down somewhere, and he carries it) or crack it by ear: slow and noisy.
  • Inside: walk into the villa, open doors with E, and read the documents you find (E).
  • Then get back to the start hill, where green smoke marks the pick-up.
  • Look for toolboxes (weapon parts) and weapon crates (rare rifles): you keep what you find. Supply caches (ammo, medical, explosives, bottles) are spread around the map.

Planes, artillery and the generator cover the sound of your shots. Tall grass hides you if you crouch or go prone. Guards who spot you run for the siren.

Testing

To try every weapon, scope, suppressor and barrel without finding them first. What you have really found is kept and comes back when you switch this off. While it's on, you can also change your loadout during a mission, from the Loadout tab. The firing range at the start has targets to shoot at.
