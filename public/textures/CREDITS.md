# Texture credits

- `forestGround.jpg`: *Forest Ground 04* by Poly Haven (polyhaven.com),
  CC0. Resized to 1024 px.
- The rock on the stones and boulders in `models/ground_detail.glb`: *Rock Boulder Dry* by Poly
  Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0. Resized to 1024 px.

The other photographs here were generated with Higgsfield for this game; the vegetation textures
in `models/tree_*.glb` and `models/ground_detail.glb` were rendered in Blender (tools/blender), and so
were the far-country panoramas `horizon_*.webp` (tools/blender/horizon.py: procedural, no outside data).
