# Mechanics worth borrowing (2026-11-06)

Design ideas only, rebuilt our own way: no names, art, music or levels from other games.
Built 2026-11-06 (tasks/todo.md); the Later ideas aren't. Effort is a rough guess. Sources are mostly secondary (write-ups of the talks, reviews, guides): the talks themselves weren't watched.

Already in the game, so not on this list: noise ring on the minimap, sound masking, bodies found, tracks and scent,
scope glint, per-mission wariness, side jobs, end ratings, counter-snipers on stands (Black Pine).

| # | Mechanic | Idea from | What's missing here | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Counter-fire: repeat shots from one spot narrow the guards' bearing, then a squad flanks it and throws grenades | Sniper Elite 5 complaints about telepathic detection; Alien: Isolation's pressure and release | Far shots give a rough bearing only; no flanking or grenades in guards.js | Medium |
| 2 | Animals and rigged alarms: flushed birds make nearby guards look that way; tin cans and glass on approaches | Hunt: Showdown sound traps | Shots already flushed birds guards notice; a herd you startle didn't count | Small |
| 3 | Shot feedback: a brief ring after each shot showing how far it carried and who heard | Mark of the Ninja, Splinter Cell: Blacklist | The minimap ring already showed shots; who heard them didn't show | Small |
| 4 | Scene clues: open doors, blood, a partner who doesn't come back | Thief, Blacklist's social awareness | Guards find bodies and tracks only | Small to medium |
| 5 | The garrison learns between missions, told on the dispatch card | Metal Gear Solid V | Wariness resets each mission | Medium |
| 6 | Per-mission challenges that unlock rewards, kept in the profile | Hitman | Ratings and side jobs, but no lasting list | Medium |

Later: a pacing layer that eases off after long pressure (Alien: Isolation); shade as binary cover at close range (Mark of the Ninja);
dummy heads to draw a counter-sniper's fire (Sniper Elite 5 has decoys in Invasion).
